# Making your label — the Easy Creator guide

You don't need design experience. This walks through the whole flow; on a
phone it works exactly the same way.

## 1. Start

From **Your labels**, press **Make a new label**. (In a hurry? "Make my
label for me" asks everything on one screen — including your vial size and
an optional QR link — and designs three finished labels for you.)

Prefer to shop by design? **Templates** in the sidebar opens the full
library of 120 designs, each previewed with your own words once you start
typing. Picking one there starts your label directly.

## 2. What are you labeling?

Pick your vial from the pictures — 10 mL, 20 mL, 30 mL, dropper bottles, or
"Something else" for any round container. Then tell us the **glass color**
(clear, amber, frosted, cobalt, or opaque) — designs that flatter that
glass get recommended first, and the 3D preview wears it.

If you know your container's exact size, choose **Yes, enter them**: wrap a
strip of paper or string once around the straight part of the vial (where
the label will sit), measure it in millimeters, and enter the height of the
straight side. That's it — every technical dimension (label size, extra
print area, safety margins) is calculated for you. Curious? "View technical
dimensions" shows the numbers.

## 3. Choose your label material

Big cards show each material — holographic cards shimmer as you move your
pointer (or tilt your phone). Each option says in plain words what it looks
like, what it's best for, how tough it is, and whether it needs a specialty
label stock. Screen previews of shiny materials are simulations — the real
film does the real magic.

Holographic and neon materials have an intensity control — from a subtle
accent to a full-effect background — and, in the editor, a **"Where should
the effect go?"** control: everywhere, just the border, the logo, the
product name, an accent panel, or the background. Whatever you pick, the
design rules protect readability automatically (text never sits raw on a
busy reflective pattern).

## 4. How should it feel?

Pick a feel — clean and clinical, luxury, bold, futuristic, botanical… or
let Forge choose. Add your brand name, product name, and strength, and say
whether you prefer light or dark labels.

## 5. What needs to fit?

Say how much information the label carries — **just the essentials**, **a
standard label**, or **lots of details** — and switch on anything special:
a QR code, a barcode, or your logo. Designs that genuinely handle your
amount of content (checked, not guessed) are recommended first.

## 6. Pick a design

Six designs appear already wearing your words, on your vial size, in your
material — each labeled (**Best match**, **Most professional**, **Most
minimal**, **Most bold**, **Most premium**, **Alternative style**) with a
one-sentence reason. Not feeling them? **Browse all templates** opens the
full library with filters and favorites (tap the heart to keep a shortlist).

## 7. Edit by typing

The editor is a form next to a live vial preview:

- **Type** and the label updates — sizes, spacing, and line breaks adjust
  themselves. Nothing can fall off the label. If something genuinely
  can't fit (say, a QR code on a very short label), the editor says so in
  plain words instead of squeezing it into an unreadable corner.
- **Toggles** show or hide optional details (ingredients, warnings, lot
  numbers, a website, a verification link, QR codes…). Turning something
  off remembers its text.
- **Your logo**: upload a PNG or JPG and it's placed and sized for you,
  reflowing with everything else. Replace or remove it any time.
- **Typography** is a feel, not a font list: pick a personality (Clean,
  Luxury, Bold, Technical…) and browse its curated pairings — each
  previewed in its own faces. You never choose font names or sizes.
- **Colors** are curated pairs that always stay readable.
- **Design** switches layouts with your words intact — a quick strip of
  suggestions plus **Browse all templates** for the full library.
- **Make it…** more premium, cleaner, bolder, more clinical, or more
  futuristic — one press, and Undo brings it straight back.
- **Quick fixes**: Balance layout, Fit everything, Bigger product name,
  Easier to read, Simplify. Every change — yours or a fix — is one
  Undo step.
- **Change material** any time; the design adapts, including where the
  shiny effect goes.
- The vial preview has Front/Side/Back/Spin buttons, a dark backdrop, and
  a flat view. What you see is a simulation of the printed result.

Want to move things by hand, add shapes, or upload images? **Advanced
Editor** opens the same label with full canvas control — and "Easy mode"
brings you back without losing anything.

## 8. Print or download

Press **Download / print** and answer one question — how will you use it?

- **Print at home** → pick your paper size and copies; you get a
  ready-to-print sheet. Print at 100% scale (never "fit to page").
- **Send to a professional printer** → a ZIP with the print-ready PDF and
  a specification sheet (size, material, finish, white-ink notes) you can
  forward as-is.
- **Download the design only** → a high-quality PNG.
- **Not sure?** → we recommend the printer package and explain why.

Before anything downloads, the label is checked and problems are explained
in plain language — most come with a one-click fix.

## 9. Build a product line

**Matching label** creates a sibling product that keeps your brand, fonts,
material, and layout — you only change the product name, strength, lot,
expiry, or QR link. Strengths can color-code automatically (5 mg blue,
10 mg purple, 20 mg red, 30 mg gold) and you can pick any colors instead.

## Tips

- Everything saves automatically, in your browser (no account needed) or
  to your account in cloud mode.
- `{{placeholders}}` in text fill from a spreadsheet in the Advanced
  Editor's Batch export — useful for lot numbers and serials.
- The compliance basics are on you: templates never invent certifications,
  approvals, or medical claims, and neither should your label.
